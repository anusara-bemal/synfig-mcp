# Example AI Prompts for Synfig MCP

## 1. Logo Reveal Animation
> "Create a 5-second 1080p animation at 24fps. Add a dark gradient background, then animate a circle with a radial blur scaling up from the center over 2 seconds with ease-out interpolation. Add a bold title layer in the middle that fades in at second 2."

## 2. Character Rig and Walk Setup
> "Inspect the active document scene tree. Find the character group layer and list its bones. Set up an inverse kinematics chain for the leg bones and keyframe a simple 24-frame looping walk cycle."

## 3. Explainer Scene
> "Create a 3-part explainer graphic: add three rounded rectangles side by side, color them with vibrant flat gradients, and attach animated text callouts above each box."

## 4. Fix My Project
> "Inspect my open project. Identify any off-screen layers or broken keyframes, align all layer durations to 120 frames, and render a preview frame at time 2.5s."
